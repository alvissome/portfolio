Finding your live status

In Boost Integrator, open Portfolio Support Email Delivery, Personal Portfolio Daily Check or Monthly Portfolio Report, then select History. In the Boost data portal, open your Personal Portfolio Watchlist module for prices and delivery records. These details require your signed-in account.

What silence means

Daily alerts send only when a condition triggers or a check fails. The monthly report is sent every month even when no alert threshold is reached.

  • No daily email: open the latest check to confirm it succeeded.
  • Failed or stale price: inspect the failure email and run record before acting on a price.
  • Email accepted: delivery history records the sending service’s acceptance; it cannot guarantee inbox placement.

The DCA Tracker sheet is retained as an archive. Its old scheduled alerts and monthly sender have been retired; its historical rows are not used by these workflows.

All schedules are Singapore time. Closing-price comparisons exclude dividends, fees and currency conversion. Private portfolio data and connection keys are not embedded in this page.
